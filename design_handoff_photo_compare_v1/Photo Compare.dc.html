4c How it works
AT IMPORT Run pose detection on the photo. You need three landmarks: nose, and both ankles. MoveNet Lightning or MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, both on-device, both free.
THE MATH Scale = target height ÷ (ankle Y − nose Y). Then translate so nose Y lands on the crown line and hip X lands on centre. Store scale, dx, dy per photo. Never touch the original file.
WHEN IT FAILS Ankles below the frame, ankle confidence under about 0.4, or a side view where one ankle occludes the other. Fall back to the crown line plus an estimated floor, flag the photo amber, let her drag. Never silently ship a bad alignment.
ONE CATCH Aligning by nose-to-ankle normalises her apparent height, which also cancels out camera distance. That is the point. But it means the card can no longer honestly claim to show height change — only shape change. Worth knowing before someone asks.
